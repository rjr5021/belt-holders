The belt goes to Dallas. 🏆

Dallas Renegades 20, St. Louis Battlehawks 16. The Renegades storm into St. Louis and take the belt back, ending the Battlehawks' 5th reign after just 5 days.

The Battlehawks had won it on May 24, but they couldn't hold on in a tight one at The Dome at America's Center. Dallas led 13–6 at the half and hung on despite a third-quarter St. Louis touchdown. The Renegades answered in the fourth to seal it, 20–16.

Belt game No. 34 is in the books. Dallas begins its 2nd reign with the UFL Belt.
