The belt goes to Dallas. 🏆

Dallas Renegades 20, St. Louis Battlehawks 16. The Battlehawks' 5th reign ends after just 5 days — Dallas takes the belt for the 2nd time.

The Renegades led 13–6 at the half and never trailed, but the Battlehawks made them sweat it out. St. Louis pulled within 13–10 after three, then made it 20–16 late, but Dallas held on at The Dome at America's Center to end the Battlehawks' short-lived reign that began on May 24.

Belt game No. 34 is in the books. The Renegades reclaim the belt.

Next up: stay tuned for Dallas's first defense.
