The belt goes to Portland. 🏆

Fire 90, Golden State Valkyries 82 (OT). The Valkyries' 5th reign ends after 29 days — they took it from the Lynx on Aug 24.

Golden State jumped out 24–10 after one, but Portland clawed back and forced overtime tied at 77. The Fire dominated the extra period 13–5 to claim the belt. Bridget Carleton led all scorers with 26, Jordan Harrison dished 10 assists, and Emily Engstler pulled down 11 boards. Gabby Williams had 17 to pace the Valkyries.

Belt game No. 1,215 is in the books. Portland's wait since 2001 is over — this is the franchise's 2nd reign.

Next up: stay tuned for the Fire's first defense.
