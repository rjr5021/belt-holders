The belt goes to Portland. 🏆

Portland Fire 90, Golden State Valkyries 82 (OT). The Fire snap the Valkyries' 5th reign at 29 days and claim the belt for just the second time in franchise history — their first time holding it since 2001.

The Valkyries jumped out 24–10 after one, but Portland clawed back and forced overtime tied at 77. In the extra frame, the Fire outscored Golden State 13–5 to seal it. Bridget Carleton led the way with 26 points, Jordan Harrison dished 10 assists, and Emily Engstler pulled down 11 boards. Gabby Williams scored 17 for the Valkyries.

Belt game No. 1,215 is in the books. Golden State's 29-day run ends at the Moda Center.
