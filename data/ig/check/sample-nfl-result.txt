The belt heads south. 🏆

Jacksonville Jaguars 35, New England Patriots 6. The Patriots' 22nd reign ends at just 7 days — they took it from the Dolphins on Sep 20 — and the Jaguars claim the belt for the 6th time in franchise history, exactly one year after they last held it.

This one was never close. The Jags shut out New England in the first quarter, then put up 14 in the second, 14 in the third, and 7 in the fourth. Trevor Lawrence went 19 of 29 for 182 and three touchdowns, Bhayshul Tuten rushed for 73 and a score, Jakobi Meyers caught 7 balls for 64 and a TD, and the defense picked Drake Maye off twice and held the Patriots to a pair of field goals.

Belt game No. 1,645 is in the books.

Next up: the Jaguars carry the belt to Cincinnati. At the Bengals, Sun Oct 4, 1 PM ET on CBS. Beat the holder, take the belt.
