The belt changes hands in Paris. 🏆

Paris FC 2, RC Strasbourg 1. Strasbourg's 43rd reign ends at 21 days after they took it on Aug 29.

Paris FC led from the start — 1–0 after the first half, 2–1 at the final whistle. Lassine Sinayoko led the attack with 5 shots, Maxime López distributed 77 accurate passes, Diego Coppola anchored the defense with 11 interventions, and Kevin Trapp made 1 save to preserve the victory. Strasbourg's Filip Jørgensen made 5 saves in a losing effort.

Belt game No. 2,960 is in the books. Paris FC takes the belt for the 2nd time, their first reign since 2026.
