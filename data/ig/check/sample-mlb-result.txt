The belt goes to Minnesota. 🏆

Minnesota Twins 6, Texas Rangers 4. Texas's 178th reign ends after just 1 day — they took it on Sep 26 — as the Twins claim the belt for the 193rd time in franchise history.

The Twins built a 5–1 lead through five innings and held on. Minnesota plated single runs in the first, third, and eighth, and added two-spots in both the fifth and the seventh. Texas answered with a run in the third, another in the eighth, and pushed two across in the ninth, but it wasn't enough.

Belt game No. 22,800 is in the books.

Next up: the Twins carry the belt out of Target Field. Details still to come. Beat the holder, take the belt.
