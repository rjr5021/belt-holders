The belt stays in New York. 🏆

New York Knicks 94, San Antonio Spurs 90. First defense of the Knicks' 204th reign, now 3 days and counting since they took it from San Antonio on Jun 10.

The Spurs led 23–13 after one and were up 42–37 at the half, but the Knicks stormed back. Jalen Brunson went off for 45 points, Josh Hart pulled down 11 boards, and New York outscored San Antonio 57–48 over the final two quarters to escape with the belt.

Belt game No. 7,529 is in the books. San Antonio's wait since 2026 continues.

Next up: the Knicks carry the belt to Philadelphia. At 76ers, Mon Oct 5, 7 PM ET on NBA TV. Beat the holder, take the belt.
