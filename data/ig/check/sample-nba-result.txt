The belt stays in New York. 🏆

New York Knicks 94, San Antonio Spurs 90. First defense of the Knicks' 204th reign, now 3 days and counting since they took it from the Spurs on Jun 10.

The Spurs led 23–13 after one and hung around all night, but the Knicks never trailed after halftime and closed it out with a 29–18 fourth quarter. Jalen Brunson dropped 45, Josh Hart pulled down 11 boards, and Mikal Bridges dished 4 assists. Victor Wembanyama grabbed 14 rebounds for San Antonio, and Dylan Harper scored 25, but it wasn't enough.

Belt game No. 7,529 is in the books. The Spurs' wait since 2026 continues.

Next up: the Knicks carry the belt to Philadelphia. At the 76ers, Mon Oct 5, 7 PM ET on NBA TV. Beat the holder, take the belt.
