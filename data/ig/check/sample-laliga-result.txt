The belt stays in Bilbao. 🏆

Athletic Bilbao 0, Deportivo Alavés 0. Second defense of Athletic Club's 76th reign, now 14 days and counting since they took it from Sevilla on Sep 5.

A goalless draw at San Mamés, but the holder keeps the belt. Neither side found the back of the net across 90 minutes. Yuri Berchiche led Athletic with 4 shots, Aitor Paredes made 89 accurate passes and 13 defensive interventions. Antonio Sivera kept Alavés in it with 6 saves, while Nicolás Valentini matched Paredes with 13 defensive interventions of his own.

Belt game No. 3,115 is in the books. Alavés last held the belt in 2026.
