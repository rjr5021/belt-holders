New champion crowned. 🏆

Atlante 4, Monterrey 2. Monterrey's 13th reign ends after just 6 days — the belt changes hands at Estadio Banorte.

Atlante led 1–1 at the half, then exploded for three second-half goals to take the belt for the first time. Eugenio Pizzuto led the attack with 3 shots, Aarón Quirós controlled the midfield with 42 accurate passes, Eduardo Tercero anchored the defense with 18 interventions, and Óscar Jiménez made 5 saves to seal it. Monterrey's Carlos Salcedo put up 63 accurate passes and 20 defensive interventions, but it wasn't enough.

Belt game No. 539 is in the books. Atlante claims the belt for the first time in club history.
