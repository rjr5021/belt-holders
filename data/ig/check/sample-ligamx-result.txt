The belt changes hands. 🏆

Atlante 4, Monterrey 2. The Potros take the belt for the first time, ending Monterrey's 13th reign after just 6 days.

Atlante led 1–1 at the half, then exploded for three in the second to take control at Estadio Banorte. Eugenio Pizzuto led the attack with 3 shots, Aarón Quirós orchestrated from the back with 42 accurate passes, Eduardo Tercero anchored the defense with 18 interventions, and Óscar Jiménez made 5 saves to seal it. Monterrey couldn't match the intensity despite Carlos Salcedo's 63 accurate passes and 20 defensive interventions.

Belt game No. 539 is in the books. Atlante claims the belt for the first time in club history.
