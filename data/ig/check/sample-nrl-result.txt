The belt stays with the Roosters. 🏆

Sydney Roosters 19, Newcastle Knights 18. First defense of the Roosters' 12th reign, now 9 days and counting since they took it on Sep 25.

The Knights led 12–6 at halftime and 18–19 after two, but couldn't find the points they needed in a scoreless second half. The Roosters' 19 first-half points held up — just barely — as they edged Newcastle by a single point at Accor Stadium.

Belt game No. 257 is in the books. Newcastle's wait since 2025 continues.
