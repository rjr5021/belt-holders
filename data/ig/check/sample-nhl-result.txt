San Jose Sharks 5, Los Angeles Kings 4 (OT). First defense of the Sharks' 41st reign, now 2 days and counting since they took it on Oct 1.

Chernyshov finished with 3 points, Macklin Celebrini dished 2 assists, and Yaroslav Askarov made 31 saves to hold off a 35-shot Kings attack.
