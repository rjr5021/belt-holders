The belt changes hands at the MCG. 🏆

Brisbane Lions 96, Fremantle 89. The Lions claim their 10th reign, ending Fremantle's 6th after just 8 days since the Dockers took it on Sep 18.

Fremantle led by a point at quarter time and held even through the half, but Brisbane found another gear when it mattered. The Lions piled on 34 points in the final term to Fremantle's 17 and seized the belt in front of the MCG faithful.

Logan Morris kicked 3 goals, Will Ashcroft racked up 31 disposals, and Josh Dunkley won 14 contested possessions for the Lions. Patrick Voss booted 3 for Fremantle, Shai Bolton gathered 26 disposals, and Caleb Serong won 17 contested possessions, but it wasn't enough.

Belt game No. 258 is in the books. The Lions are back on the throne.
