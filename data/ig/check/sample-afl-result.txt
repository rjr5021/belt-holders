The belt returns to Brisbane. 🏆

Brisbane Lions 96, Fremantle 89. The Lions claim their 10th reign, taking it from Fremantle after just 8 days and ending the Dockers' 6th stint with the belt.

Fremantle led by 9 at three-quarter time — 72–62 — but the Lions exploded for 34 points in the final term to steal it at the MCG. Will Ashcroft racked up 31 disposals, Josh Dunkley dominated with 14 contested possessions, and Logan Morris booted 3 goals. Caleb Serong led Fremantle with 17 contested possessions, Shai Bolton had 26 disposals, and Patrick Voss kicked 3 goals, but it wasn't enough.

Belt game No. 258 is in the books. The Lions last held it earlier this year.
